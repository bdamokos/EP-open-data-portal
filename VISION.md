# Project vision

Parliament explorer is a proof-of-concept website for having fun with open data. It uses the European Parliament’s existing open data APIs to connect people, questions, speeches, votes, documents and legislative procedures, uncover new insights and invent engaging ways to explore them.

The aim is to have fun, experiment and make data that can feel dry or difficult to navigate accessible and interesting. Exploring the activity of Members of the European Parliament is a way into understanding the work of Parliament and, more broadly, the European Union.

## Explore connections

Go out, find data that is open and free, and bring it into the app when it makes exploring more fun. Combine parliamentary data with other open APIs to discover connections across domains.

Try new and unusual ways of presenting the results: connect a member’s questions and speeches around a topic, follow a legislative dossier through its participants and stages, or show how themes develop over time. Let one discovery lead to another.

## Process public data into useful forms

Server-based processing is part of the approach. Data and documents received from public APIs may need parsing, extraction, conversion or indexing before they become useful for exploration. This can include turning difficult-to-read PDFs, XML files and other published documents into searchable text or structured formats.

## Use scraping deliberately to close gaps

APIs are the preferred source. In limited cases, deliberately scrape public websites where an API is absent or omits a needed relationship. For example, reading a public webpage can establish how item A corresponds to item B when the API does not expose that connection.

Collect only public information. Never bypass authentication, access controls or other security protections, or undermine the security of EU websites.

## Phase two: AI enrichment

Phase two will explore how local or publicly available AI models can further analyse and enrich the data, find new connections and create new ways to explore it.
